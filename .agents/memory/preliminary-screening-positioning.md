---
name: Preliminary screening positioning
description: Product-claim boundary for PhotonLint’s current rules and bend geometry checks.
---

PhotonLint must present all current results as preliminary geometry screening, not official foundry DRC, PDK validation, tape-out readiness, or sign-off.

**Why:** The active values are public reference estimates or engineering overrides, not NDA-gated official foundry decks. Bend findings are a polygon-vertex circumradius heuristic, not reconstructed centerline or parametric-curve verification.

**How to apply:** Keep the non-sign-off disclaimer and rule-source provenance in product UI, API result payloads, downloadable reports, slides, and marketing copy. Any official-compliance claim requires a licensed source and independent validation against the foundry flow.
