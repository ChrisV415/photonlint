**PhotonLint** is a lightweight, cloud-native **Design Rule Checking (DRC)** software tool built specifically for Silicon Photonics and Photonic Integrated Circuit (PIC) designers. 

Think of it as the "Grammarly for Photonic Chip Layouts." Just as Grammarly checks your writing against grammatical rules before you publish, PhotonLint checks a photonic chip's physical layout against a foundry's manufacturing rules before it goes to the fab.

Here is exactly what it does and why it is critical:

### 1. What It Actually Does (The Mechanics)
*   **Ingests Layout Files:** A photonics engineer uploads their chip layout file (typically a `.gds` or `.oas` file) to the PhotonLint web app.
*   **Applies Foundry Rules:** The software parses the geometric shapes of the waveguides, ring resonators, and couplers and checks them against the specific Process Design Kit (PDK) rules of a target foundry (e.g., GlobalFoundries 45SPCLO, AIM Photonics, or Tower Semiconductor).
*   **Flags Physical Violations:** It instantly identifies geometric errors that would ruin the chip during manufacturing, such as:
    *   **Minimum Spacing:** Waveguides placed too close together, which causes optical crosstalk (light leaking from one channel to another).
    *   **Minimum Bend Radius:** Waveguides making turns that are too sharp, causing the light to scatter and leak out of the silicon.
    *   **Grid Snap:** Coordinates that do not align perfectly with the foundry’s manufacturing grid (e.g., a 1nm grid), which causes lithography errors.
*   **Generates a Report:** It outputs a clean, downloadable CSV or PDF report detailing exactly where the errors are, their severity (Critical vs. Warning), and how to fix them.

### 2. The Problem It Solves (The Value Proposition)
In the semiconductor industry, sending a chip design to a foundry for manufacturing is called a **"tape-out."** 
*   **The Cost of Failure:** If a layout violates a foundry's DRC rules and slips through to the fab, the manufacturing process will fail. The startup loses the **$100,000 to $500,000+** cost of the wafer run and suffers a **3 to 4-month delay** waiting for the next manufacturing shuttle.
*   **The Legacy Tool Problem:** Currently, startups are forced to use heavy, expensive, on-premise Electronic Design Automation (EDA) tools (like Synopsys or Cadence) that cost hundreds of thousands of dollars a year and require dedicated IT infrastructure. 

**PhotonLint acts as an insurance policy.** It catches these catastrophic, expensive errors in seconds, directly in the browser, for a fraction of the cost of legacy software. It allows agile deep-tech startups to iterate on their photonic designs rapidly without risking a six-figure manufacturing mistake.
