Good framework, and it points at something concrete and current, not abstract — let me apply it directly rather than just restate it back.

**Who's the realistic "Microsoft Office" here?** Three candidates, and they're not hypothetical:

1. **KLayout** — already free, already the incumbent, already what your target users have their DRC scripts hand-rolled in. If it ever gets a cleaner cloud/CI mode, "PhotonLint" as a separate product has no reason to exist — it's just a feature KLayout was missing.
2. **gdsfactory** — this is the sharper one, because it's also your *outreach target*. It's Python-native, open-source, has real momentum, and if its maintainers add a built-in `check()` function, your entire value prop (Python + Shapely DRC checking) becomes a redundant wrapper around something already in the tool people are using anyway.
3. **The foundries themselves** — this one isn't hypothetical, it's already happening: Spark Photonics partnered directly with Tower Semiconductor to make Check Mate DRC an approved part of using Tower's PDK. That's the exact Da Vinci Systems pattern — DRC checking getting absorbed as a bundled feature of "using this foundry," not sold as a standalone product.

**The uncomfortable part: this connects directly to the SDK decision we just made.** Open-sourcing the DRC engine is good for trust and distribution — but if gdsfactory is the platform that could swallow you, publishing your engine as a clean, pip-installable, permissively-licensed package makes it *easier*, not harder, for someone to fold the functionality straight into gdsfactory itself, with no reason left to use "PhotonLint" by name. That's worth sitting with, not glossing over — the BSL license (rather than plain MIT) at least creates friction against someone directly re-hosting it as a competing *service*, but it doesn't stop someone reading your open-source engine and reimplementing three similar checks natively inside gdsfactory in a weekend.

**What "aiming past" actually looks like here:** don't build "a DRC checker." Everything already in the product — the layout viewer, the PDK override system, the CI/CD integration, the report generation — is *already* reaching past DRC alone, whether that was deliberate or not. The bigger category to aim at explicitly is something like **"the CI/DX layer for the whole photonic tape-out-readiness workflow"** — DRC is the first check, not the whole product. That's a materially harder thing for KLayout, gdsfactory, or a foundry to casually absorb as one feature, because it's a workflow/platform claim, not a single-function one.

Concretely, this argues for prioritizing the CI integration and the "tape-out readiness" framing over the SDK release timing — get PhotonLint embedded in someone's actual pipeline (the sticky, hard-to-rip-out position) before the open-source engine gives a bigger platform an easy blueprint to build the same single feature natively.
